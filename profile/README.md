## Enzo's — AI home discovery

[**Enzo's**](https://enzos.ai) is a US home-discovery site. Describe the home you want in
plain language, or browse by architectural style, feature, and mood, and get a clean visual
grid of real homes for sale. Every home links out to its original source listing.

Enzo's also connects to AI assistants. Through its agent API, ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini can
search homes, give an honest "is this a good buy?" read, and surface below-market homes from
live, cited data instead of stale guesses.

- Home search: https://enzos.ai
- For AI assistants: https://enzos.ai/for-agents
- How the predictions work: https://enzos.ai/how-we-predict
